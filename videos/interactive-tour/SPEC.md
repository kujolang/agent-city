# Agent City interactive tour

Show how to judge an agent's answer by walking through the actual evidence for
one release-notes tool. The viewer can watch the whole story or jump to a room.

- 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps. Seven chapters in 65.8 seconds.
- Pixel artwork fills the left panel. The right panel uses a warm paper ground,
  a 74 px headline and one supporting evidence label. Mint marks the current
  stop. Keep the product pixels crisp and all labels still while they are read.
- Use a 32-frame Motion cubic Bézier push into a building, a short dissolve to
  the room, and a 23-frame return to the map. Give the archive a wider reveal.
- Start on the city, then visit Dispatch, Library, MCP Center, Workshop, Dojo
  and Archive. Open each room on its first spoken line. Pull back after the
  final phrase. Finish on the saved mission evidence.
- Siren narration uses Eleven v4. A quiet original 92 BPM score supports it.
  Short stereo cues mark zooms, with a distinct chime for the Dojo result.
- Check the opening, every entry and exit, the rendered proof image, and the
  chapter player at desktop and mobile widths.
